# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Multi-Agent Research Assistant to be welcoming, safe,
and constructive. Everyone who contributes in good faith should be treated
with dignity, regardless of identity, background, experience, education, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and honesty;
- respect different viewpoints, experiences, and clearly stated boundaries;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback gracefully;
- take responsibility for mistakes and make a sincere effort to repair harm;
- credit other people's work and protect confidential information; and
- prioritize the health of the community over winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention;
- insults, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or stereotyping;
- sexualized language or attention inappropriate to the project;
- sharing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, deliberate misrepresentation, or evasion of moderation;
- plagiarism or intentionally failing to credit contributed material; and
- sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or other collaboration.

## Reporting a concern

Report possible violations privately through a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Axeloooo). Do not include
sensitive or identifying information in a public issue.

The maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as possible.
They will limit disclosure to the people needed to understand the incident and
respond safely. Anyone handling a report must avoid conflicts of interest and
protect the reporter from retaliation.

## Enforcement

Responses will reflect the severity, impact, and history of the behavior. They
may include a private clarification or warning, temporary limits on project
participation, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Maintainers may
skip intermediate steps when necessary to protect the community.

Where appropriate and safe, resolutions may include acknowledging impact,
apologizing, correcting the record, or agreeing to clearer boundaries. The
maintainers retain discretion to choose the response that best protects the
people and spaces involved.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, project
discussions, and other spaces managed by the project. It also applies when a
person officially represents the project elsewhere.
